How it works

Lintel gives the booking site a simpler layout. It runs in your browser while you’re logged in to your own account.

1. Log in as usual

Sign in on the normal booking site. Lintel never sees your password.

2. See the whole day

Each row is a room and each column is half an hour. Blue means booked.

3. Pick and confirm

Select your slots and press Confirm. Nothing is booked until you do.

What you can do

Book several slots at once

Select a few slots, even across rooms, then review them before you confirm.

Book lab equipment

Browse equipment by type and lab, and see each item’s own hours.

Laptop or phone, light or dark

Use it in Chrome, on iPhone or on Android. Your bookings, credits and cancellations are all in one dashboard.

What Lintel does, and doesn’t do

You should know what you’re installing, so here it is plainly.

  • Never sees your passwordYou log in on the booking site itself, and Lintel stays out of it.
  • Never books by itselfBookings and cancellations only happen when you confirm them.
  • No account, no ads, no analyticsIt has no server of its own and doesn’t track you.
  • Only works on the booking siteIt runs on that one website and nowhere else.
  • Your data stays on your deviceIt keeps a few things locally so pages open quickly.
  • Sends reports only if you choose“Report a problem” opens a form that you can edit, and it’s sent only if you submit it.

Questions

Is this made by SIT?

No. Lintel is an independent student project, and it isn’t affiliated with or endorsed by SIT. If you have a problem with your booking itself, contact SIT as usual.

Is it free?

Yes, it’s free, with no ads or sign-up.

Who can use it?

Anyone with an SIT account who can already use the booking site. It only shows what you could already see there.

Are my bookings real?

Yes. Lintel uses the same booking system, so your bookings are the same as ones made on the normal site.

Why does the phone version need another app?

Phone browsers don’t support Chrome extensions, so Lintel runs as a small script. On iPhone that uses the free Userscripts app, and on Android it uses Tampermonkey in Firefox. You can remove it at any time.

How do I stop using it?

Remove it from Chrome, Userscripts or Tampermonkey, and the booking site goes back to normal.

Something’s not working. Who do I tell?

Use Report a problem. Please don’t include other people’s names or IDs.
